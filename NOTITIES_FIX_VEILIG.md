## What is new
### 1. Losknuffelen (unstuck) — `feature/vadsrijk/kern/veilig/`
- Key **U** (`key.guhs.vr_vast`, category Vadsrijk, rebindable; client `kern/veilig/client/VeiligClient`) and
  **`/guhs vadsrijk vast`** (everyone). Payload `guhs:vr_kern_vast`.
- 3 s channel (pink swirl + chimes); moving > 0.75 block or getting hit (kern `Schade.NA_KLAP`, damage > 0) stops it.
  60 s cooldown (in memory, per player). Blocked in an active boss fight: inside an `Arenas` arena whose boss is alive, or
  within `losseStraal()` of a loose boss -> "Niet tijdens een bazengevecht! Loop door de deur naar buiten (of val flauw)."
- Target (`Losknuffelen.doel`): the safe spots within 64 blocks (2D), sorted by distance x (POI/campfire 0.7, path 0.85, grid 1.0);
  the spot right where you stand is skipped; up to 32 nearest are checked in the world (floor below, feet+head free, no
  water/lava, not in an active fight; unloaded chunks: generated spots are trusted). Nothing -> around your last "good" ground
  position -> your checkpoint (last campfire) -> `Flauwvallen.standaard`.
- Stuck hint "Vast? Druk op [U]" (actionbar, keybind shown live) + first-time tip card (`vadsrijk.kern.tip.vast`):
  - no movement out of a 3-block radius for 20 s AND `Opsluiting.ingesloten` (local flood fill, survival rules, radius 8,
    max 3000 spots) -> every 30 s while it lasts;
  - feet below **y 40** -> every 15 s (the lowest walkable floor of the map is y ~53; the Kloof bottom is y 29-33).
- Below **y 20** (= fell under the map onto the bedrock at y 0, or out of the map edge): rescued at once, no channel, no cooldown.
- Hooks: `KernRegistry.register` -> `Veilig.register()`, `KernRegistry.payloads` -> `Veilig.payloads(r)`, `KernClient.init` ->
  `VeiligClient.init(modBus)` (one line each). Texts in `tools/features/vadsrijk_kern.py` (TEKSTEN) + both lang files.

### 3. Walkability audit — `tools/vadsrijk/audit/`
- `audit.py` (+ `tegellezer.py` fast numpy tile reader, `blokklassen.py` block classes). ~70-80 s for the whole map, ~6 GB RAM.
- Model: dense block-class volume (bedrock at y 0 like the flat generator, temple doors open), per-column "runs", edges with
  MC rules in half blocks (step up 1, fence/wall tops 1.5, slabs, fall any height, swim up + 1 block out, ladders/vines/
  cloud streams, city lifts + airship as links). Seeds = campfires + arrival.
- Finds: pits/rooms you can't leave (one line per place where you fall in), falling under the map (hollow underground),
  stair-flight ends that don't connect (roof stairs `dakpan`/`tentdoek` ignored), path steps of 2+ blocks, doors with a
  blocked side / not reachable, trees/mushrooms on paths, grass/flowers as floor under a building roof, water > 10 swims from
  a way out (open sea listed separately), POIs not on the network.
- Output: `AUDIT_VADSRIJK.md` (project root), `tools/vadsrijk/_cache/audit_vadsrijk.png` + `audit_<gebied>.png` crops (cache is
  git-ignored). Debug: `AUDIT_DEBUG=1` also writes `_cache/audit_debug.npz` (runs + reach flags).

## Main findings on the current map (see AUDIT_VADSRIJK.md for all coordinates)
- **De Kloof** (the gorge with the river, Zwamzoet east -> Klodderweide -> past the Zwamtempel path): one trap of ~11 400
  columns (y 29..96) + a second of ~5 300 columns: you fall in from hundreds of edge blocks and there is no way back up.
  Needs exits (stairs/ladders) or fences along the edge.
- **Druipgrotten**: the caves connect to the hollow underground of the map (8 entry points): you fall onto the bedrock at
  y 1 (now: auto-rescue). And the cave nodes (`grot_knopen.N1..N4`, `npc_mopje`, `grot_ingangen.1/2`) are reachable but you
  can't get back out ("je komt er niet meer weg"): the cave entrances are 2.5+ block drops.
- Zwamtempel: a 121-column pit at the path switchback (283 138 -358), a 1-column hole at 301 133 -350, a stair at
  322 149 -374 that doesn't connect.
- Zwamzoet: 34 door findings, 24 of them doors in walls with both sides blocked (decorative, some sunk 1 block into the
  ground), 5 with the back blocked, 5 upstairs doors not reachable; the
  `gebouw.*.binnen` interiors are not reachable; the ordetempel front stair has a 2-deep gap at z -27 (x -6..6).
- Knuffelkamp: the arrival stair (x -1..1, z 1119) ends 1 block short; vuurtorenkelder is a pit; 24 tent floors are grass.
