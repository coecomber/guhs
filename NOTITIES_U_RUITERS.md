# NOTITIES U-RUITERS (round U, 2026-11-06, branch 110-u-ruiters from eaee06d08)

The user's wish (FEEDBACK_RONDE4, "rijdende guhs"): the mounts must become thematic and iconic and bring the world to life. Guhs
riding the Pluimguh run around zone 3 (the tree's stairs, Honingtak, the Pluimstal, the ground) and ~2 in zone 1 (curiosity).

## What is built (package `feature/vadsrijk/ruiters/`)
- **Entities** (both `noSave`, MISC, never hurt, not pushable, no collisions):
  - `vr_ruiter_pluim` = `RuiterPluim extends economie.RijdierEntity`: the real Pluimguh (model, colours, run/hop/idle animations,
    pattern + tack layer through the economie `RijdierRenderer`), no AI, no gravity, noPhysics; only a `RuiterGuh` may ride it.
    Client: dust from its feet while running + now and then a feather puff in its own colour (only within 24 blocks = LOD).
  - `vr_ruiter_guh` = `RuiterGuh extends npc.RijkGuh` (like the caravan's HandelGuh): real guh + clothes, sitting pose on the
    saddle (attachment y 1.98), looks at players, waves now and then (RijkGuh's idle). Also used standing as the groomer.
- **Routes** (`RuiterRoute`, `RuiterRoutes`): `data/guhs/vadsrijk_ruiters/routes.txt`, GENERATED by
  `tools/vadsrijk/ruiters/routes.py` from the REAL map tiles: anchors from the zone-3 generator geometry (riding spiral
  `rij_punt_lev`, inner stair `trap_lev`, Honingtak ring r 58, ground ring r 172, training oval) or POI ids / the caravan road;
  snapped to stand cells (3 blocks of air), A* between anchors (steps <= 1, half slabs = 0.5, no doors/water/fences, prefers path
  blocks and the middle of a path), then string pulling with a body-width check (0.6 to each side free at 1-2 blocks up, no
  railing at feet height). Every straight piece between kept points is walkable and y is linear (half-step stairs = a ramp).
  13 routes, 22 riders:

- **Movement** = a pure function of game time (`RuiterRoute.stand(t, k)`): no state, nothing saved, riders of a route spread over
  the period, rest 5 s at both ends (back-and-forth), speed 0.18-0.30 blocks/tick on the flat, x0.8 on slopes, x0.65 on
  stairs (so the run animation on the flat, a hopping trot on stairs). The manager moves the entity kinematically each tick, y
  smoothed (max 0.3/tick), yaw eased. **Cannot get stuck**; >6 blocks off -> snap onto the route; a spot in a non-ticking chunk
  -> the pair is removed and comes back on its route later ("teleport to next waypoint when out of sight").
- **Manager** (`Ruiters`, instance `Beheer`, the world's one = `Ruiters.WERELD`, Vadsrijk dimension only):
  - budget **14** pairs in the dimension, nearest-to-a-player first, within 80 blocks flat / 48 up-down;
  - one map key `route#k` -> one pair; spawn only when `isPositionEntityTicking && areEntitiesLoaded` (not in the load window);
  - join guard (EntityJoinLevelEvent LOWEST): a rider entity the manager didn't just spawn is refused;
  - EntityLeaveLevelEvent: a rider that leaves without being removed (ghost in an unloading chunk) is discarded next tick;
  - sweep every 5 s: every rider entity no pair owns is discarded. Self-contained: does not need u-dubbel (compatible with its
    ghost sweeper: the types are `noSave()` builders, not `shouldBeSaved` overrides).
- **Looks**: deterministic per rider key (same look every time it reappears). Pluimguh colours zone 3: roze/mint/lavendel/honing
  (often), wolkwit/sterrennacht/kristal/lampion (sometimes), never goud; zone 1 only the 4 common ones. Pattern sometimes
  (vlekjes/streepjes/hartjes/sterretjes), tack dekje/bloemenkrans/belletjes/lampionnetjes (zone 1: dekje). Guhs: normal/mint/
  choco/snow/pluis/bloesem/starry, jockey cap / straw hat / zonnehoedje / ... + scarves, sometimes the jockey jacket. Zone-1
  courier: jockey cap + backpack + Vadsrijk scarf. Groomer: choco guh with farm hat + garden apron.
- **Click** (rider or Pluimguh): a line in chat, "<name>: ...". Zone 1 mostly "Ooit krijg jij er ook één!"; zone 3 six lines +
  a stairs line, a waiting line, the groomer line. Names invisible (no nametag clutter), used in the line.
- **Dev**: `/guhs vadsrijk ruiters lijst | tp <route> [ruiter] | aan | uit | spawn` (spawn = a demo rider doing laps r 5 round
  you for 2 minutes, random look).

## Decisions I made myself
- Kinematic movement instead of pathfinding (the caravan's proven pattern): smooth on stairs, can never get stuck, zero
  pathfinding cost. The step-height-2 of player mounts is irrelevant for kinematic movement; the planner allows steps <= 1.
- The Bijentak villages (Zoemrust, Raatweide, Molenhoek plaza aside) are only reachable by a ladder from Honingtak: that branch
  rider goes to the Lianenbrug instead. Molenhoek plein is reachable and has its own rider.
- The training oval is cut on its east end by a big root (also a walkability point for players? the dirt path is dotted there):
  the loop goes round it (one anchor skipped).
- Riders have no visible nametag.
- Budget 14 and radius 80 (guess, untested for FPS on the user's machine: tune in `Ruiters.BUDGET/ZICHT`).

## Tests
- `runGameTestServer -Pgt=VadsrijkRuiters`: **all passed** (2 mine + the default env test).
  - `routesKloppen`: all 13 routes present, in their zone box, no piece > 18.5 blocks, none steeper than a stair, the spiral and the
    inner stair climb ground/Schorsstad -> kruin 1, the timeline never moves a rider faster than its speed (no teleports), riders
    of a route are spread out, z1 >= 2 and z3 >= 15 riders.
  - `geenDubbelen`: a test manager on a test route + a stable route: 4 pairs, rider sits on its Pluimguh, groomer stands next to
    one; a foreign rider entity is refused by the join guard; "reload" (manager forgets its pairs) -> the sweep removes exactly the
    8 old entities and one pair per rider remains; still 4 after 40 ticks of riding; hidden -> 0, shown again -> 4; budget 1 -> 1.
- No AutoCheck client run (looks are for the user in the dev client: saddle height, speeds, density).
- Note for integration with u-dubbel: the riders are RijkGuh subclasses with an empty npc id (like the caravan's HandelGuh), and the
  types are noSave builders; Dubbel's heuristic scan may list them as "same npc id" groups: harmless, but exclude
  `vr_ruiter_guh` there if it reports them.
