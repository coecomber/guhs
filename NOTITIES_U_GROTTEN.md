### Map side: `tools/vadsrijk/grotten/`
- **`grot.py` is the engine.** The geometry is 2.5D (a floor + ceiling per column) plus explicit 3D parts.
  - **Elements:**
    - `kamer`: an elliptic room, flat floor, dome ceiling;
    - `gang`: a tunnel along waypoints with floor heights in 0.5 steps (slabs);
    - `meer`: a lake with an ice rim;
    - flooded tunnels with air pockets (water level per element);
    - `trap`: a spiral stair, a 2-wide ring round a 3x3 glowing pillar, 6 up per turn. Every lane step is at most 0.5, the head room is 5, and there is a fence guard at the top.
  - **The shell:** 2 blocks round all air/water. The inner layer gets the theme's wall blocks; the outer layer only fills empty/soft cells. Both happen ONLY below each column's natural ground top. Mouths stay open, and nothing is built in the open air by accident.
  - **Ground-top fallback:** a column without ground (a giant mushroom stem, a trunk) takes the highest ground in a 9x9 neighbourhood.
  - **Zone 3:** keeps the World Tree's own wood/roots where they cross a cave above head height.
  - **The hook:** `grot.pas_toe(vol, tx, tz, id_fn, namen, zone)` works on the pre-hollow tile volume, in world coordinates.
  - **Built-in checks (while building):** neighbouring dry floor cells differ by at most 0.5, and water never sits next to cave air.
  - **Cave-in-cave warning:** a cave cell carved into an existing enclosed hollow is printed as "grotcellen in een al bestaande holte". None were found.
- **`grotplan.py`** holds the five caves: themes, elements, POIs, the entrance structures and the deco.
  - Every cave is written in its zone's world-local frame through `indeling.py` (zone 3 `z3(rx, rz)` = relative to the trunk).
  - `indeling.py` is loaded under its own module name, because zone 4's kit has a different `indeling` module.
- **`grotcheck.py`** checks the REAL tiles. It walks from the entrance (z4: from the exit chimney) with half-block steps only inside the cave. Checks:
  - every POI standable and reachable;
  - every cave cell can walk back;
  - no cave column above the void;
  - the fishing anchors have water.
- **Hooks in the makers** (add-only, marked `U-GROTTEN`):
  - `maak_kaart.py`: pre-registers the zone-1 states before the opaque lut, applies the cave before `hol`, and adds `grot_module()`;
  - `zone2/maak2.py`: the same, with the world tile index;
  - `zone3/maak3.py` and `zone4/maak4.py`: in `Werker.tegel` before the return;
  - `zone5/maak5.py`: after `tegel()`.
  - u-hekken's verbind pass runs over the written tiles afterwards (the fences/walls of the caves connect).

### Game side: `feature/vadsrijk/grotten/`
- **`GrotData.java`** is GENERATED by `tools/features/vadsrijk_u_grotten.py` from the plan. It holds per cave:
  - the area box with a y range: room ceilings only, so the surface above and the top of a spiral are not "in the cave";
  - the levels and the biome id;
  - all POIs;
  - the Ambacht groups.
- **`Grotten.java`:**
  - **Areas:** `grot_<id>` (with y range), pushed in front with `Gebieden.voegVooraan` at server start and again for 2 s after it, so they win over the zone lists. Lang: `vadsrijk.kern.gebied.grot_<id>`.
  - **Biomes:** `guhs:vadsrijk_grot_<id>` ("Het Vadsrijk - De Zwamgrot"), through `Grotten.biome` at the top of `VadsrijkBiomen.index(x,y,z)`.
  - **Discovery card:** `Haken.GEBIED_BETREDEN`. The first time, 25 ticks after kern's area title, a gold title card (name + "Grot ontdekt!" + subtitle), an amethyst chime and a chat line.
  - **Elite + mobs** through kern `Spawners` (cave mode):
    - `grot:<id>#elite`: max 1, elite chance 1.0, respawn 6000 ticks;
    - `grot:<id>#mob`: 3 cave mobs in the big room, respawn 600.
    - Bonus via `Haken.VERSLAGEN`: every helper gets coins (150·zone²+100 brons), plus a ZELDZAAM piece on the first kill. On top of the elite's normal drops.
  - **Treasure:** walk up to the chest (2 blocks). It works only once the cave's puzzle (`grot_<id>`) is solved; otherwise you get a locked message + sound, at most every 10 s. The treasure gives:
    - an EPISCH piece at the elite level;
    - 600·zone²+400 brons;
    - a title card.

    It comes once per player, and glitter shows while it is not taken.
  - **Book:** category "Grotten" on zone pages 1-5, 4 entries per cave: ontdekt, elite, puzzel, schat. The treasure entry shows BEZIG once the puzzle is done.
  - **Ambacht:** the 10 groups go into `AmbachtPlekken.GROEPEN`. Their POIs `grot_<id>_amb_n` resolve through `Grotten.plek`.
  - **Sound:** `GeluidPlan.EXTRA` maps `grot_<id>` to a streek with the cave bed. Themes: druipgrotten / splinterkloof / wortelduister / lampionkust_avond / hoogvads. The bed is "grot", except "wortel" for zone 3.
  - Dev commands: `/guhs vadsrijk grot lijst | tp <id> [plek] | reset <id> | elite <id> | kaart <id>`.
- **`client/GrotSfeerClient.java`:**
  - particles round you inside a cave box (spores, snow sparkles, root dust, drips, cloud ash);
  - every 5-14 s a drip or ping somewhere above, with a softer echo.
- **Puzzles:** 5 extra puzzles in `tools/features/vadsrijk_t_puzzels.py`, anchor source `"grot"`, and `PALET[4]`/`PALET[5]`. The json is generated; the generator validated the light and push puzzles.
- **Gametests:** `VadsrijkGrottenGameTests`, run with `-Pgt=VadsrijkGrotten`.
  - `data`: 5 caves in zones 1-5; every POI; area + biome at the stand POIs and not 40 above; the puzzle and its anchor; 2 Ambacht groups; spawners; the sound bed.
  - `spelerKant`: discovery; the treasure locked, then once; the elite marked; the book 4/4.

## Decisions I made myself
- **Placement:**
  - z1: under the Sporenwoud, west of the forest path. The toadstool spot was searched for a clear r 7 circle (stems all round).
  - z2: in the Galmtop, the high mountain, so there is 30+ blocks of rock over the halls. Mouth west over the slope.
  - z3: the northern ring of the island between r 125 and 185. The Hart dungeon (r 112, rooms at 6-152°) and the Wortelduister hall (r < 86) stay untouched.
  - z4: not in the lagoon (the Zeeslurf arena + zeeslurf_grotje are there) but under the east rim.
  - z5: the whale's head, x < 2560. It stays clear of the Buikgangen (x >= 2704), the Schuilnest (x >= 2588), the Wolkwaldroom (x 2585-2619) and the fin shafts.
- **Spiral stairs as entrances** (z1, z3, z5 and the z4 exit): 20-28 blocks down without a long ramp, and they look inviting from above (a toadstool, a stump, the blowhole).
- **The z4 dry exit:** a coral chimney on the rim. It is the "way out" of the walkability standard, and also a way in for players who do not dive.
- **Treasure needs the puzzle:** it ties the cave's parts together. The treasure is a walk-in chest (an ordinary chest block without loot; the reward is a loot bundle, like the zone-1 secrets).
- **Puzzle ids are `grot_<id>`.** The z1-z3 cave puzzles also show in t-puzzels' "Puzzels en geheimpjes" category of that zone (the toolbox lists every puzzle of a zone). They count there too: a small double count, accepted.
- **Elite = an existing mob type** spawned as an always-elite with affixes. The names in chat/book are cave names ("Oude Zwamwachter"); the mob's own nameplate stays the type name.
- **Ice only on the 1-deep lake rim** (a frozen shore): nobody gets trapped under ice. The open middle is for fishing.

```
python tools/vadsrijk/maak_kaart.py --zone 1 "--alleen=-9,1;-9,2;-8,1;-8,2;-7,1;-7,2"
python tools/vadsrijk/maak_kaart.py --zone 2 "--alleen=13,-19;13,-18;13,-17;14,-19;14,-18;14,-17;15,-19;15,-18;15,-17"
python tools/vadsrijk/maak_kaart.py --zone 3 "--alleen=20,-12;20,-11;21,-12;21,-11;22,-12;22,-11"
python tools/vadsrijk/maak_kaart.py --zone 4 "--alleen=34,-10;34,-9;35,-10;35,-9"
python tools/vadsrijk/maak_kaart.py --zone 5 "--alleen=38,-9;38,-8;39,-9;39,-8"
PYTHONPATH="tools;tools/features" python tools/features/vadsrijk_u_grotten.py
PYTHONPATH="tools;tools/features" python tools/features/vadsrijk_t_puzzels.py
python tools/vadsrijk/grotten/grotcheck.py
```
`--alleen` lists with negative numbers need the `=` form. `python -c "import grot; print(grot.tegels())"` in `tools/vadsrijk/grotten` prints the tiles. A full regen (`maak_kaart.py --zone alle`) picks the caves up automatically.
