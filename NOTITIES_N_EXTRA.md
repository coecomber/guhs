# N-EXTRA (branch 110-n-extra, from 110-k-int e5ab2935e)

## 1. Critters around the cheered-up (pink) Vahoegnir
- `feature/vadsrijk/vriendjes/DraakVriendjes` (registered in `VadsrijkFeature`): a server tick finds pink dragons in the Vadsrijk
  near players (zone-3 boss `Vahoegnir` with `splinters() >= 5`, plus anything handed to `DraakVriendjes.volg(entity)` by a later
  scene) and gathers up to 32 critters within 25 blocks while a player is within 48 blocks.
- Critters = existing Guhs models as `Z3Figurant` props (no AI, no hitbox, never hurt, never saved): pluisvinkje + kaasmeesje
  (flutter in circles, land on the ground to hop/peck, perch on his back/neck/tail), pluiseekhoorntje (existed already; hop, sit,
  eat, dig), a few guh-konijntjes (pink/white/brown), pink pieppiepmuisjes (new texture `textures/vadsrijk/vriendjes/muisje_roze.png`,
  generated by `tools/features/vadsrijk_vriendjes.py`), knabbelvlindertjes (4 colours).
- Birds/butterflies fly in from afar; ground critters pop out with a petal puff + heart. When he moves fast (takes off) everyone on
  his back flutters off. When he is gone / nobody near: birds fly off, the others hop away with a poof.
- Where the pink dragon exists: ONLY in his ending scene (`Z3Scenes.vahoegnirEinde`, splinter 5 at t=30 until he flies off along
  the beam and is discarded at t=420, ~20 s), story and Droomsteen "herinnering" alike. There is no resting pink dragon afterwards
  (the hq05 nest flashback and the zone-1 flyovers use the grey flyover dragon). A future resting pink dragon only needs
  `DraakVriendjes.volg(it)`.
- Perch spots are estimated from the draak model (x1.6, idle root +5 units); wings are folded up in idle, so no wing perches.

## 2. Pet nameplate "<pet> van <player>"
- `Huisdieren.roep` sets the custom name `Huisdieren.naamVan(h, player name)` = translatable `vadsrijk.huisdieren.naam_van`
  ("%s van %s") with the pet's name key + the player's name. Vahoegje is a normal pet, so it is included.
- Shown like a vanilla named mob: only while you look at it (crosshair ray within 8 blocks; the pet isn't pickable so
  `HuisdierClient.bekeken` aims itself), hidden with F1. No custom name = no nameplate (the raw key bug stays fixed).
- Lang added in `tools/features/vadsrijk_huisdieren.py` + nl_nl/en_us.
