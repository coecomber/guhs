# SCHEIDING_RAPPORT: survival <-> Vadsrijk (round Y, slice y-scheiding)

The user's choice was to test and close leaks ("alleen testen en dichtzetten"), with no origin stamp on items. Every channel below was checked against the code at 65cd32467 and against the pack's mod list (`guhs-server/guhs-pack-repo/mods`: appleskin, architectury, bluemap, chunky, ferrite-core, **ftb-essentials**, ftb-filter-system, ftb-library, ftb-quests, ftb-teams, geckolib, jade, jei, journeymap, **lootr**, modernfix, mouse-tweaks, sodium, spark). The pack has no backpack mods, no Curios/Accessories, no gravestone mods and no waystones.

The fixes are in `src/main/java/nl/juiced/guhs/feature/vadsrijk/scheiding/`. The tests are in `VadsrijkScheidingGameTests` (`runGameTestServer -Pgt=VadsrijkScheiding`).

**Allowed bridges** are not part of this slice: FTB deco rewards (y-ftb), the Vadsrijk-vitrine (y-vitrine) and outfits as cosmetics (y-cosmetica).

## Channel notes

**Gametest server and the dimension.** The tests drive the swaps through the same calls kern's reconciler makes: `Inventaris.naarRijk/naarThuis`, then `Lichaam.verzoen`. The border rules are tested through their decision functions. When the gametest server has `guhs:vadsrijk`, the tests also do the real teleports (the log line `Y-SCHEIDING: the Vadsrijk dimension is ...` says which).

**Channel 9, a horse ridden into the Vadsrijk.** Vanilla already dismounts a player who teleports across dimensions; `Grens.losLaten` makes sure. A leashed mob stays on its side; vanilla keeps the leash data, and it reconnects when you come back.

**Channel 10, why "Guhs code on the stack" and not "Vadsrijk code".** Erfenis `terug`, the Guhpixel lobby and similar features bring a player back to where they stood, which can be inside the Vadsrijk (they left from there). Refusing those would strand the player. The guard is meant for other mods. No Guhs code outside `feature/vadsrijk` sends a player into the Vadsrijk otherwise.

**Channel 13, the exceptions.** `Thuispost.RIJK_CODE` is `feature/vadsrijk` plus `event/KeepOnDeathHandler` and the gametest helpers. Vanilla pickups (no Guhs frame on the stack) still fill the live inventory, as they should.

## Open (decisions for the user or other slices)

- **Vadsmunten -> survival items, by design (not changed here):**
  - Mollie in the Knuffelkwartier (`huizen/Menus.koopBijMollie`) sells *vanilla building blocks* for Vadsmunten into the home inventory: cherry planks, bookshelves, lanterns, a pink bed, glass, Guh furniture and the Vadsrijk banner.
  - The Vadsrijk Post (`economie/Post`, `Cosmetica`) delivers bought things home: Guh clothes and deco (= cosmetics, allowed), Knutselwinkel and Werkplaats "post" goods, an ambacht keepsake and heart glasses.
  - Under "nothing but deco via FTB, the vitrine and cosmetics", the user should decide whether Mollie's vanilla blocks and the non-cosmetic Post goods stay.
- **FTB quest rewards claimed inside:** that is y-ftb's job. FTB item rewards claimed by hand would land in the live Vadsrijk inventory until y-ftb blocks claiming inside. FTB code is not Guhs code, so `Thuispost` does not catch it.
- **Non-op `/anvil`, `/crafting`, `/smithing`, `/stonecutter` (FTB Essentials) inside:** they work on the Vadsrijk inventory with the Vadsrijk xp pool (normally 0). Nothing crosses the border, but Vadsrijk materials can be crafted into vanilla items inside. Not refused, because it is not a leak; add them to `Grens.GEWEIGERD` if unwanted.
- **Op `/invsee` of a player's ender chest or inventory from the other side:** ops only.
