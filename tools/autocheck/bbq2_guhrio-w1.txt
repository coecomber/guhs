# bbq2 (guhrio-w1): Super Guhrio wereld 1, de binnentuin (levels 1-1 and 1-2), in the real client.
# NOT run by the slice (a slice starts no game client): this is for the merge step's visual check.
# Builds the whole castle unturned in the sky with its corner at 6000 100 6000 (/guhs guhrio bouwkasteel), like
# bbq2_guhrio-engine.txt. A cell (s, row) of a level is then at:
#   1-1 (west wing, runs south):  x 6019, y 127 + row, z 6011 + s      start (s 2, row 3) = 6019 130 6013
#   1-2 (east wing, runs north):  x 6107, y 127 + row, z 6106 - s      start (s 2, row 3) = 6107 130 6104
# In the garden (rows 0..9) you walk on row 3 (y 130), one floor up (rows 10..19) on row 12 (y 139).
# (The sections "1-1" and "1-2" of bbq2_guhrio-engine.txt were written for the engine's practice lanes: with this slice
#  merged their teleports land in THESE levels, one row too high. This script replaces them.)
# Look at: the picture must be a clean side view of ONE floor (the garden: grass, hedges, the painted wall with hills that
# have eyes and pale trees; one floor up: clouds, treetops, the top of the wall, pale towers and clouds with a snoet far
# away). Nothing on the painted wall may look like something you can stand on. The tips appear above the panel (yellow)
# while the level was never finished. The plants on the lawn are crosses you walk through. The jump: a step of 2 and a
# pit of 3 must be easy (guhriosprong measures it) - if not, the levels need lower steps, not a different check.
# The gag: at the flagpole of 1-2 Pad-guh stands in front of a little gate; a second after the "gehaald" line he says
# "Bedankt! Maar de prinses is in een ander kasteeldeel, njeg." in the chat and above the panel.
# Run:  gradlew runAutocheckClient -PautocheckScript=tools/autocheck/bbq2_guhrio-w1.txt -PautocheckTimeout=30
