# bbq2 (paleizen) AutoCheck: the three Mika palaces as try-out copies in the overworld, their characters and the Worstzwijntje,
# and the real copies in the Guhbarbecuether. Run:
#   gradlew runAutocheckClient -PautocheckScript=tools/autocheck/bbq2_paleizen.txt
# (commands: see autocheck.txt; "/guhs paleizen bouw <structuur>" is a dev-only command: it puts the building in front of
# you, its way in at your feet, and the questlines treat it as a real copy; "/guhs verhaal stap <lijn> <speler> <n>" sets a step)
# Template coordinates -> world, for a copy built at x y z:
#   brugpaleis: world = (x - 2, y - 26, z - 15) + template    woonblokken: (x - 31, y - 13, z - 53) + template
#   stal:       (x - 40, y - 7, z - 12) + template
