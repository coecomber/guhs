# biomes3 wereld-dal: the finished Klaterdal in a real generated world, for the client pass (nothing here was ever seen in a
# client by the slice itself). To look at: broad pink terraces with white rounded rock faces, the river with its low
# cascades and (where it is found) the tall waterfall with foam and mist, pale moss, guhbloesem trees that keep away from
# the water, crooked little trees on boulders, guh-bamboe, petals on the pools, the lighter turquoise water, the pale sky,
# falling petals (many more in the rain and during a gust of wind, which comes about every two minutes with a wind chime).
# Listen: the babbling ambience loop, the koto music, the chime.
# Run:  gradlew runAutocheckClient -PautocheckScript=tools/autocheck/bio_wereld_dal.txt -PautocheckTimeout=40
