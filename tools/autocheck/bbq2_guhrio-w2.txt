# bbq2 (guhrio-w2): world 2 of Super Guhrio, "de kelders" (levels 2-1 and 2-2), in the real client.
# NOT run by the slice (a slice starts no game client): this is for the merge step's visual check.
# Builds the whole castle unturned in the sky with its corner at 6000 100 6000 (dev command /guhs guhrio bouwkasteel), so
# castle coordinates + (6000, 100, 6000) are world coordinates. The cellar's row 0 is y 104: you walk in row 4 (y 108), the
# rooms above the ceiling have their floor in row 12 (you stand at y 117).
#   2-1 (west wing): the lane is x 6019, it runs SOUTH: z = 6011 + s.   start block 6019 108 6013
#   2-2 (east wing): the lane is x 6107, it runs NORTH: z = 6106 - s.   start block 6107 108 6104
# Look at: a clean side view of every part (nothing in front of the lane, the whole height of the main lane in the picture),
# the teal ground and brick ceiling with its keystones, the painted wall (lamps on the pillars, barrels, painted pipes, the
# egg over the door), the Hapbloemen in their pipes, the two platforms and the island, the red cage round the vadsmunt, the
# falling blocks, the lift and the high ledge; in the rooms: the picture must show the room and not the lane below it
# (the coin cellar, the eierkamertje with the egg on its hay, the black warp room with 3-1 / 3-2 / 1-1 in gold over the
# three gold-and-purple warp pipes). The hatching scene: black bars, the player's stand-in walks to the nest with the egg
# (a flat egg picture that faces the camera) over its head, the egg hops into the nest, wobbles twice, the screen goes
# dark, Guhshi sits on the nest, hops, the subtitles read "Krak...", "Njeg?", "Njeg! Mama!". After it: Guhshi on the nest,
# then under the player. The warp: the player stands at the start of 3-1 with the message in the chat.
# Run:  gradlew runAutocheckClient -PautocheckScript=tools/autocheck/bbq2_guhrio-w2.txt -PautocheckTimeout=30
