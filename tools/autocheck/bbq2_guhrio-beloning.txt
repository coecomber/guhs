# NOT run by the slice (a slice starts no game client): this is for the merge step's visual check.
# Builds the whole castle unturned in the sky with its corner at 6000 100 6000 (dev command /guhs guhrio bouwkasteel), so
# castle coordinates + (6000, 100, 6000) are world coordinates: the forecourt's floor is y 126, you stand at y 127.
#   Pad-guh 6051 128 6120 (on his counter), the highscore block 6074 127 6119, the two green pipes: their mouths at
#   6045 128 6121 (left, by the balustrade) and 6068 128 6120 (right of the path), the flagpole 6080 128 6120,
#   the floating ?-block 6058 130 6121; the tower room: Prinses Perzikguh 6063 143 6063, Guhshi 6068 143 6065.

# cap and blue vest), the highscore board (the floating text reads against the black panel), the flagpole's foot / pole /
# flag, the pipes (a round green pipe with a rim), the talking screens of the shop (three buttons, no overlap), a pipe trip

# tower room (peach on the wall, cake table, the princess with crown and golden hair, Guhshi with his own red saddle and
# NOT the leather one), the five outfits on a guh, Guhshi's flutter and tongue.
