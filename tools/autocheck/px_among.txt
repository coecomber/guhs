# guhpixel slice "among" (visual QA, dev only; run by the merge step): Among Guhs, the engine. The lobby with the
# Kapitein-guh and the Logboek-guh, the queue screen, a round as crew (HUD, the ship, the guh NPCs in their suits, a task
# panel, a meeting) and a round as the Mika (the pillow, the Saboteerkaart, a vent). Run:
#   gradlew runAutocheckClient -PautocheckScript=tools/autocheck/px_among.txt -PautocheckTimeout=20
# Buttons are pressed by their Dutch label (guhstaal nl). Screens of a round cannot be opened without a round, so the script
# plays: /guhs px among speel normaal crew|mika starts one alone, at once, with that role.
section setup
guhstaal nl
/gamemode survival
/time set noon
/weather clear
wait 40

section the lobby: the Kapitein-guh and the Logboek-guh
/guhs px lobby
wait 60
/tp @s 0.5 100 -22.5 180 0
wait 20
shot among_lobby_kapitein

section the queue screen
/guhs px among wachtrij
wait 20
shot among_wachtrij gui
# the leader picks Lastig
press Niveau: Normaal
wait 10
shot among_wachtrij_lastig gui
gui close
wait 5

section a round as crew: the start, the HUD, the ship
/guhs px among speel normaal crew
wait 80
shot among_crew_start
/guhs px among rollen
wait 5
shot among_crew_rollen
# look around the Kantine: the table with the button, the guhs in their suits
look 180 10
wait 10
shot among_crew_kantine
look 0 10
wait 10
shot among_crew_kantine_zuid

section a meeting (the dev command calls one)
/guhs px among vergader
wait 30
shot among_vergadering_bespreken gui
# a statement: "Ik was in..." and then a room
press Ik was in...
wait 10
shot among_vergadering_kies_kamer gui
press de Slaapzaal
wait 200
shot among_vergadering_uitspraken gui
wait 260
shot among_vergadering_stemmen gui
press Overslaan
wait 300
shot among_vergadering_uitslag gui
wait 200
shot among_na_vergadering

section the tasks done by the dev command: the crew's bar fills, the round goes on until the NPCs finish
/guhs px among taken
wait 20
shot among_crew_taken_klaar
/guhs px among einde
wait 60

section a round as the Mika: the pillow, the map
/guhs px among speel normaal mika
wait 80
shot among_mika_start
# the Saboteerkaart in the hand, and use it
/item replace entity @s weapon.mainhand with guhs:among_saboteerkaart
wait 5
useitem 2
wait 20
shot among_saboteerkaart gui
gui close
wait 5
/guhs px among einde
wait 60
/guhs px huis
