# bbq2 (bestaand) AutoCheck: the Wachter-guh at a real Spiesburcht and the Knuffelmaker-guh at a real Mika-grillpaleis, with
# what Bezetting brings there (the wachthokje, the four bridge fires, the naaihoek) and what only the player sees (a fire that
# burns, the Mikakruid, an empty cage with its plush in the naaihoek). Run:
#   gradlew runAutocheckClient -PautocheckScript=tools/autocheck/bbq2_bestaand.txt
# (commands: see autocheck.txt; /guhs bestaand stand | wis | vuur <0..3> | kooi <0..2> | ga <hokje|brug0..3|tuin|kooi0..2|naaihoek>,
#  /guhs wereld bezetting = the copies around you get what they miss at once)
# Look for: the hokje next to the statue with the Wachter-guh in it and the Zielig lantaarntje above his plume; a cold fire

# naaihoek in the fourth corner of the first floor with the Mikas' sign; cage 0 empty and the Knuffelguh in the naaihoek.
