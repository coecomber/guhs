#   gradlew runAutocheckClient -PautocheckScript=tools/autocheck/bbq2_ring-knipogen.txt
# (commands: see autocheck.txt; the op commands used here: /guhs ringknipoog speel <naam> | stand | vergeet
#  (feature/ringknipoog/RingKnipoogCommands.java: speel plays a wink in the frame of the building the player stands in,
#  whether or not it was seen), and the chapters' own try-out builders, dev runs only: /guhs ringh2 bouw, /guhs ringh3
#  bouw, /guhs ringh4 bouw, /guhs ringh6 bouw, /guhs ringsausuman toren.)
# Every wink is 5 to 10 seconds; the shots are taken at its beats. What to look at, per wink:
#   baltoguh  the council ring of Guhvendel: Baltoguh trots in from the bridge, everybody turns to him, his line, he leaves
#   kloon     the west forecourt of the mine: the professor hops up to Guhdalf at the gate (the wall behind them), "Nee."
#   spiegel   the dell: Mieuwguh floats over the rim BEHIND Guhladriel (is she in the picture?), then the look into the
#             bowl: a tiny Mewtwo-guh on the water with two kaasknabbels (size, does it stand ON the water?)
#   stitch    Sausuman's hall: the 626-guh comes in through the door, the button on the Guh Oven (a block display: is it
#             on the oven's face?), his paw, the confetti, Sausuman's line
#   boris     the Derde Richel: Boris flaps in along the wall (wings: his cutscene animation "vlieg"), his line, Sam-guh's
#   sjokkel   the feast: Sjokkel in the foreground, the party behind him, everybody cheers
#   kistje    a ?-block over a lane: the chest pops out of the block, Pad-guh pops out of the ground two cells on
#   sjekel    the council ring of Guhvendel right after the fellowship was formed: Pippguh steps up to the ring on the stone
#             (is the ring between him and the camera, not over his face?), his long line (three lines of subtitle: do
#             they fit in the bar, in Dutch and in English?), the wide shot with everybody staring at him and "Huh?", a
#             second in which nothing at all happens, and the cheering going on
# NOT run by the slice itself (no game client in phase 3 so far): this is for the final visual check.
