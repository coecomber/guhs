# Het Knabbelpark (1.4.2) AutoCheck: the nearest park in the Guhmensie from a few viewpoints (do the walks follow the hills,
# does anything float or sit buried?), the plaza, the statues, the deer pen, the parkwachter; then the walk check.
# Run:   gradlew runAutocheckClient -PautocheckScript=tools/autocheck/park_knabbelpark.txt
# (commands: see autocheck.txt; /guhs park keur is the park's own walk check, its lines go to the report via chat)

section setup: the Guhmensie, a clear noon
/gamemode spectator
/execute in guhs:guhmension run tp @s 0 140 0
wait 60
/time set noon
/weather clear
/gamerule doDaylightCycle false
/effect give @s minecraft:night_vision infinite 0 true
renderdistance 16

section the park from the sky and from its sides
tplocate guhs:guhmension knabbelpark
wait 100

section the plaza, the statues, the deer pen, the hut (template coordinates of the plaza)
camtpl 16 18 -6 0 55
waitrender 300
shot park_plein_boven
camtpl 16 4 6 0 15
waitrender 200
shot park_fontein
camtpl 16 10 -28 180 25
waitrender 200
shot park_beeldentuin_richting
camtpl 52 12 16 90 30
waitrender 200
shot park_hertenkooi_richting
camtpl -18 10 16 270 30
waitrender 200
shot park_parkwachter_richting
camtpl 16 30 70 180 40
waitrender 300
shot park_paden_zuid

section the walk check
/guhs park keur 200
wait 40
quit
